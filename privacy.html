Privacy policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

This policy covers the Trend Vault browser extension and the Trend Vault Pinterest app. It describes exactly what is read, where it goes, and what is never collected.

In one paragraph

The extension reads the product page you are viewing so it can write marketing copy about that product. Nothing leaves your computer until you press a button. There is no Trend Vault server, no analytics, no tracking and no advertising. The only third party involved is the AI provider you choose and configure with your own key.

What is read

What When
The Amazon product page you are viewing: product title, brand, category and feature bullets, plus the page URL. Automatically on Amazon product pages, so the panel is ready when you open it. This is local only and happens on no other site.
Your own Pinterest data, if you connect a Pinterest app: your pins and their destination links, and metrics for your own account — impressions, saves and outbound clicks. Read-only. Only after you connect, and only when you open the data view. The extension cannot read anyone else's Pinterest data, and cannot post, edit or delete anything.

Where it goes

Nowhere, until you press a button. The product brief is held in memory and in per-tab session storage. When you press Generate, that brief is sent to the AI provider you configured, as part of the prompt. Which provider that is, is your choice:

ProviderWhat it receives
Google GeminiThe product brief and prompt
OpenAIThe product brief and prompt
OpenRouterThe product brief and prompt
GroqThe product brief and prompt
A custom endpoint you specifyThe product brief and prompt
A model on your own machine (Ollama, LM Studio)Nothing leaves your computer

Your Pinterest data is never sent to an AI provider. It is read to rank your own products and is displayed to you.

What is stored, and where

Everything is stored in your own browser profile, using the browser's extension storage. Nothing is synchronised to other devices, and there is no Trend Vault account.

StoredWhy
Your AI provider API keyTo call the provider you chose. Stored locally, never synced.
Your settings and preferencesLanguage, tone, audience, keywords, disclosure wording, affiliate tag.
Generated copy, up to 40 entriesSo reopening a product does not spend your API quota again. Oldest entries are removed first.
Products you have generated copy for, up to 500So their performance can be shown per product. Removed oldest-first when the limit is reached.
Product information for open tabsHeld in session storage only, which the browser clears when the session ends.
Pinterest app credentials and access tokens, if you connectTo call the Pinterest API on your behalf. Read-only scopes. Removed completely when you disconnect.

You can clear the generated-copy cache at any time from the extension's settings, and disconnect Pinterest from the same page. Removing the extension removes all of it.

What is never collected

  • No browsing history, and no pages other than the Amazon product page you are viewing.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no data sold or shared with anyone.
  • No passwords. The extension never asks for your Amazon or Pinterest password, and the Pinterest connection uses Pinterest's own authorisation screen, which the extension cannot read.
  • No data used for creditworthiness or lending purposes.

Children

The extension is a marketing tool for adults running an affiliate business. It is not directed at children and does not knowingly handle their data.
